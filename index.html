Crystal: Match 3 Puzzle is a focused, colorful puzzle game. Swap neighboring pieces, make matches, and use special pieces to clear locked spaces and blue cover layers.

See the gameRead privacy policy
The loop

Simple rules, satisfying decisions.

The first nine levels introduce the board gradually, from locked cells to blue base layers and mixed objectives.

Match three

Swap adjacent crystals to make a line of three or more matching colors.

Make a special

Long lines, square matches, and shaped matches create pieces with bigger effects.

Clear the board

Spend a limited number of valid moves to remove the level's target cells.
